What does not change your bill
  • The Relay Proxy or daemon mode. We count the SDKs behind the proxy, not the proxy. Neither changes your count or your rate.
  • How many flags you have, or how often you evaluate them. 10 flags and 10,000 flags cost the same. Backend requests do not count.
  • Pod size. CPU and memory do not count. How many copies run, and for how long, does.
  • Reconnects and rolling deploys. We add up connected minutes, so a reconnect continues the same time.
  • Your browser and mobile SDKs. They never create a connection. They count as monthly active users.
Your first week after you buy
  • Set an application ID on every service. Otherwise your usage page says "Unknown" and you cannot see which service is using connections.
  • Point laptops and CI at the local flag mirror. The LaunchDarkly dev server serves flags locally, so those minutes are free.
  • Share one SDK client per process. Create it once at startup, not per request or per worker thread.
