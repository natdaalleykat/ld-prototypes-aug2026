Foundation expansion: suggest the next product at the moment it solves a problem

Prototype · Natalie Friedman · August 2026 · Reconstructions on current production pages, not screenshots · Mechanics verified against the product codebase
Premise. 99% of Foundation customers use flags only, even though Experimentation, Observability (including Session Replay), and a starter allowance of Guarded Rollouts are in the plan they already pay for. Interruption marketing won't move them; the play is to catch the moment when the adjacent product solves the problem in front of them, the value is self-evident, and the friction is provably low (we detect their SDK, their telemetry, and their tracked events before suggesting).

The ladder. The three products adopt in an order: telemetry first (one config block at install time), because it auto-creates the error metrics that make a guarded rollout zero-setup, and guarded rollouts teach the metric-plus-rollout mechanics that make a first experiment a small step instead of a leap. Every moment below nudges one rung.
Moment 1: rollout configured, inline EXPERIMENTATION
The user builds a 25/75 percentage rollout. An inline card in the targeting canvas points out they are one metric from an A/B test with this exact targeting, included in their plan.
Moment 2: rollout saved, modal EXPERIMENTATION
Same offer as Moment 1, but as a save-confirmation modal after they commit. They just took a measurable risk in production; peak motivation, higher intrusion. A/B these two.
Moment 3: flying blind on a live rollout OBSERVABILITY
The account has zero telemetry (like 99% of Foundation customers), and a rollout just went live. The Monitoring page shows evaluations climbing next to empty error and session panels: real users are on the new experience and nothing shows what they see. One setup fills the page in.
Moment 4: the retro insight, consent-first AI CASE STUDY
The banner states a config-derived fact (3 rollouts split traffic with no metric attached) and asks permission; Vega drafts the case study only when the user clicks. Drawer pivots to drafting an experiment on the live rollout.
Moment 5: personalized empty state EXPERIMENTATION
A destination, not a discovery surface: flags-only customers stay on flag paths and never wander here. Its job is converting the clicks Moments 1, 2, and 4 send, landing on their own flags as ready-to-run candidates pre-matched to events they already track.
Moment 6: the giveaway, announced GUARDED ROLLOUTS
Every account includes 5 guarded rollouts and nothing in the product says so until the allowance is half gone. A Recommended tag and one inclusion line in the rollout mode picker the user already sees, with the auto-created error metric pre-selected when telemetry exists.
Moment 7: observability at install time OBSERVABILITY
The single lowest-friction moment: the user is already in their init file. One checkbox extends the install snippet with the o11y plugins, and unlocks Moments 3 and 6 downstream. Precedent: the experimentation snippet checkbox already shipped on this page.
Every page has a "Why this moment" button in the bottom switcher with the trigger, the gating rules, and the risk to test. Numbers in the mocks (traffic, lift, error counts) are illustrative.
